# Privacy Policy for StudyJug

**Effective Date:** 24 November 2026

StudyJug is a browser extension designed to help you remember vocabulary by underlining your saved words on web pages and displaying your own definitions. Your privacy is important, and StudyJug is built to keep your study data completely local to your browser.

## 1. What information is collected/stored?

StudyJug does **not** require an account, nor does it collect personal identification information, browsing history or analytics.

All data you create or configure inside StudyJug is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). This includes:
* Language pack names, enabled/disabled states, and underline color choices
* Vocabulary words/phrases, optional phonetics, definitions, and starred status
* Unfinished word drafts and selected text saved via the right-click context menu
* Display and search preferences (such as showing phonetics and search scope)

## 2. How is web page access used? 

When language packs are enabled, StudyJug's content script runs locally inside your browser tab to scan text on a web page for words matching your saved vocabulary list, underline those words, then display your saved definition card when you hover/click an underlined term. 

When you use the right-click **"Save ___ to StudyJug"** option or open the extension popup, StudyJug reads only the text you have highlighted on the active tab so it can pre-fill the word form for you.

* All text scanning and matching happen strictly on your device.
* StudyJug does **not** record, log, or transmit the web pages you visit or your browsing history.

## 3. Data sharing, third parties and external servers

* **No External Servers:** StudyJug does not send your vocabulary packs, definitions, or web page content to any external server or cloud database.
* **No Third-Party Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.

## 4. Managing and deleting data

You remain in full control of your data at all times:
* **Inside the Extension:** You may edit or delete individual words, clear unfinished drafts or delete entire language packs directly within the StudyJug popup.
* **Complete Removal:** Uninstalling StudyJug from Chrome permanently removes all locally stored extension data from your browser.

## 5. Changes to this policy

If future updates to StudyJug introduce new features that change how data is handled (such as optional cloud sync or dictionary lookups), this Privacy Policy will be updated prior to releasing those changes.
